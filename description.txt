Terraria Performance Overhaul (TPO) — diagnostics-first performance mod.

Development build 0.3.0: measurement tools only. TPO does not change gameplay and applies no optimizations yet.

- Rolling frame-time statistics (FPS, mean, p50/p95/p99, max), CPU draw and world-update time, process memory/GC indicators.
- Optional compact overlay.
- /tpo bench <scenario> [seconds] [warmup] records a benchmark to CSV files.
- /tpo stats, /tpo (build identity).

All diagnostics are off by default: Settings > Mod Configuration > TPO Client Settings.
